# Earth maps

Day, night and cloud maps by Solar System Scope, based on NASA imagery.

Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Downloaded 2K JPG maps, converted to WebP at quality 85 for delivery (no visual retouching):
- https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_daymap.jpg
- https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_nightmap.jpg
- https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_clouds.jpg

The renderer combines day/night lighting and cloud coverage. Orbital distances and satellite sizes are illustrative, and time is accelerated. GEO shares Earth's rotation speed and equatorial plane. LEO examples use inclined planes and faster motion.

Orbit reference: https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Transportation/Types_of_orbits
